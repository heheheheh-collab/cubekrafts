# Cubekrafts — Go-To-Market & Sales Playbook (DEALER B2B SaaS)
### "The quoting & catalog operating system for local modular-kitchen dealers"
*Owner: Head of Sales & Growth · Rewritten: 29 Jun 2026 · Currency: INR · Stage: Pre-revenue · Model: vertical B2B SaaS (we sell tools to dealers, not kitchens to homeowners)*

---

## Honest Verdict on the Shape Change (GTM Lens)

**The founder asked for a pressure-test, not a pep talk. Here it is.**

### What genuinely got EASIER (the thesis is partly right)
1. **Customers are now countable and reachable.** Instead of fighting Livspace for anonymous homeowners, you sell to a finite, addressable set of modular-kitchen dealers you can list by name, call, and visit. No demand-gen CAC war. This is a real, structural improvement in GTM tractability.
2. **Revenue is recurring (MRR), not one-shot.** A retained dealer compounds; a closed kitchen doesn't.
3. **You can do founder-led sales.** B2B SMB SaaS at this stage is a phone + WhatsApp + demo motion. A solo founder can run it. No ad budget required to start.

### What did NOT get easier — and is now the KILLER risk
**Indian SMBs are brutally hard to convert to PAID software.** This is not a vibe; it's the documented pattern:
- The market is a **"precision play, not a volume play"** — uniform-readiness assumptions produce **low conversion and high churn** (Ken Research). SMB tech spend is "tightly aligned to immediate operational value" — i.e., they pay only for things that obviously make/save money *now*.
- **Low ARPU + high service expectations** is a structural trap (Kae Capital): if you must hand-hold every dealer for hours and they'll only pay a few hundred rupees/month, the math collapses.
- The graveyard is real: **Khatabook, OkCredit, Dukaan** got *millions* of free SMB users and still struggled to monetize. Free adoption ≠ revenue. A kitchen dealer's honest default is **"why pay? WhatsApp + Excel + a borrowed PDF catalog is free."**

**So the GTM did not get easier overall — it traded an unwinnable demand-CAC problem for a monetization/WTP problem.** That's a *better* problem (you control the variables, you talk to real humans), but it is not solved by the shape change alone.

### The single thing that must be TRUE to make this GREEN
**The wedge cannot be a workflow tool — it must be a money-in tool.** Indian SMBs don't pay to organize their work; they pay for *more revenue*. So the product must lead with **lead-gen / "we bring you customers"**, with quoting + catalog as the thing that becomes indispensable *because* it's where the leads land and get converted. If dealers pay because Cubekrafts demonstrably *wins them kitchen orders*, WTP is unlocked and ₹2-3k/month is trivial against one ₹3L kitchen. If the pitch is "organize your quotes faster," you will get free signups and near-zero paid conversion — the Khatabook outcome.

**The honest WTP-unlock wedge:** **Land on lead-gen as the hook (free or near-free), make the quoting+catalog tool the rails those leads run on, then charge for the rails + lead volume.** This also seeds your Act-2 consumer marketplace organically — the dealers' real quotes and catalogs become the supply graph.

### The second risk to name: activation, not just payment
Even free, dealers won't *use* it unless it slots into how they already work (WhatsApp). If quoting isn't faster than their current copy-paste, they churn in week one — paid or not. **WhatsApp-native quoting is non-negotiable**, not a nice-to-have.

### 🟡 VERDICT: YELLOW (a strong YELLOW — closer to GREEN than the last two shapes)
This is the **most fundable, most solo-founder-executable shape so far** — reachable customers, recurring revenue, no ad war. But it does **NOT clear to GREEN on the thesis alone**, because the entire outcome hinges on one unproven thing: *will these dealers pay?* Indian SMB-SaaS history says most won't, for a workflow tool. **It clears to GREEN if and only if the wedge is money-in (lead-gen-led) and you can show, in the first 8-10 pilots, that ≥30-40% convert free→paid because the tool brought them orders.** Prove that conversion in 2-3 cities and this is GREEN. Until that one number exists, it is YELLOW — promising, but the monetization risk is unretired.

## Executive Summary

- **Sell dealers a money-in tool, not a workflow toy.** Lead with "we bring you kitchen customers + the fastest way to quote them on WhatsApp." Quoting/catalog/pricing engine is the indispensable layer the leads flow through. This is the only framing that beats "WhatsApp + Excel is free."
- **ICP = growing 2-10 person dealers who are LOSING quotes** (slow turnaround, inconsistent pricing, leaking follow-ups) — NOT one-man carpenters (no pain budget, won't pay) and NOT big branded showrooms (have their own systems).
- **Land free on lead-gen + WhatsApp quoting; charge once it produces orders.** Free tier removes the "why pay" objection; paid tiers monetize lead volume + multi-user + pricing/catalog power. Realistic INR pricing ₹0 / ₹1,999 / ₹4,999 per month.
- **The motion is countable, founder-led B2B:** build a named prospect list (file 05), run outreach → free signup → activation (first real quote sent) → paid. No CAC war.
- **North Star = Paying Dealers Retained ≥3 months (net of churn).** Realistic solo 90-day target: 60-80 free signups, 25-30 activated, **8-12 paying, ₹20-50k MRR**, with a measured free→paid conversion rate that decides GREEN/YELLOW.

## 1. ICP — who feels the pain enough to PAY

| Segment | Pay? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **★ Growing 2-10 person dealers losing quotes** (the ICP) | **Yes** | They get more leads than they can quote fast; lose deals to slow/inconsistent quotes; owner is the bottleneck. Acute, money-shaped pain. |
| One-man carpenters | No | No pain budget, lowest WTP, churn instantly. Free-tier only, never the wedge. |
| Mid showrooms (10-30 staff) | Maybe (later) | Some have ad-hoc systems; sell to them in Act 2 once you have proof. |
| Big branded/franchise (Livspace etc.) | No | Have corporate systems; conflicted. Avoid. |

**ICP trigger signals to qualify on:** runs Meta/Justdial/IndiaMART lead-gen already (proves they buy growth), has ≥2 people doing quotes, quotes in Excel/PDF/WhatsApp manually, complains about follow-up leakage. These are the dealers in file 05 marked ⚡.

## 2. The WTP-Unlock Wedge (how we beat "WhatsApp + Excel is free")

**Free is the competitor. So our paid value must be MORE MONEY, not less hassle.**

Sequence:
1. **HOOK (free): Lead-gen + WhatsApp quoting.** "Sign up free — we'll route homeowner kitchen inquiries to you AND give you the fastest way to send a branded quote on WhatsApp." Money-in framing. Zero friction to say yes.
2. **STICK (activation): the catalog + pricing engine becomes their daily rails.** Their SKUs, finishes, hardware prices, and templates live in Cubekrafts; every quote runs through it; every lead lands in the inbox. Switching back to Excel now *loses* them speed and leads.
3. **CHARGE (paid): meter the money-in.** Cap free lead volume / single user / basic catalog. Charge for more leads, multi-user (so their team quotes), advanced pricing rules, branded quote PDFs, and analytics. They pay because the tool is now *where their revenue happens*.

**Why this clears the WTP trap:** a dealer closing even one extra ₹3L kitchen/month will not blink at ₹2-5k/month. The objection dies the moment the tool is attributable to an order. Anchor the sale on **"₹4,999/month vs one lost ₹3L kitchen."**

## 3. Pricing Tiers (INR/month) + rationale + realistic churn

| Tier | Price | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free (Starter)** | ₹0 | Land everyone; remove "why pay" | WhatsApp quoting (limited templates), single user, basic catalog, **limited lead inbox (e.g. 3 leads/mo)** |
| **Pro** | **₹1,999 / month** (₹19,999/yr) | The core ICP | Unlimited quotes, branded quote PDFs, full catalog + pricing engine, **higher lead allotment**, follow-up reminders, 2 users |
| **Business** | **₹4,999 / month** (₹49,999/yr) | Multi-person dealers scaling | Everything + multi-user team, advanced pricing rules, lead analytics, priority lead routing, dedicated support |
| **Lead add-on** | **₹150-400 / qualified lead** | Any tier wanting more volume | Overage homeowner leads beyond tier cap |

**Rationale:** keep entry deliberately low (₹1,999) — Indian SMB price ceilings are real; you want *conversion*, not ARPU heroics early. The lead add-on is the WTP unlock — it scales revenue with the dealer's appetite for growth and ties price to money-in. Annual prepay (2 months free) fights churn and funds you.

**Realistic churn (be honest):** expect **monthly logo churn of 5-8%** even on paid (Indian SMB SaaS reality), spiking if onboarding is weak. Mitigations: (a) activation = first real quote sent within 48h of signup (hard onboarding KPI), (b) lead-gen dependency makes leaving costly, (c) annual plans. Net retention only turns healthy once lead volume genuinely drives their pipeline — which is why money-in must be real, not theater.

## 4. The Repeatable Outreach Motion (countable B2B)

It's a finite named list now (file 05), so run it like a sales machine:
- **Channels:** WhatsApp (primary — these dealers live there), phone, in-person for high-value pilots, IndiaMART/Justdial/Instagram DM to source.
- **Cadence (solo):** ~10 new dealers/day + follow-ups; ~50 touches/week.

### Outreach — WhatsApp (copy-paste ready)

> We help modular kitchen dealers like {CompanyName} (1) get homeowner inquiries and (2) send branded quotes on WhatsApp in minutes instead of hours.
> ✅ Free to start — first leads + quoting on us
> ✅ You only pay once it's bringing you orders

### Outreach — Email (copy-paste ready)

> Cubekrafts is a quoting + catalog + lead tool built only for modular kitchen dealers. We route homeowner inquiries to you and let you send branded quotes on WhatsApp in minutes.
> Free to start; you pay only when it's winning you orders. Can I onboard {CompanyName} this week (10 min, I'll load your catalog)?

### Follow-up sequence
Day 0 WhatsApp+email · Day 2 "want me to send a sample lead?" · Day 4 call + live onboard · Day 7 "{peer dealer} sent 12 quotes this week with us" · Day 12 share an anonymized homeowner lead · Day 20 "closing onboarding slots in {City} Friday."

**Target:** 150-200 dealers contacted → ~40% sign up free (low friction) → 60-80 free → activation funnel below.

## 5. Conversion Funnel & Metrics

```
Outreach ──► Free signup ──► Activated (first real quote sent) ──► Habit (5+ quotes / lead converted) ──► Paying ──► Retained 3mo+
```

| Stage | Definition | Target conv. |
|---|---|---|
| Outreach → Free signup | Account created | 35-45% (low friction) |
| Signup → Activated | Sends 1 real quote / receives 1 lead within 48h | **50-60%** (the make-or-break KPI) |
| Activated → Habit | 5+ quotes OR 1 lead converted to order | 40-50% |
| Habit → Paying | Hits a paywall they value (leads/users) | **30-40%** ← *the number that decides GREEN* |
| Paying → Retained 3mo+ | Still paying at month 3 | 70-80% (i.e., ~5-8%/mo churn) |

- **North Star:** **Paying Dealers Retained ≥3 months (net of churn).** It captures the only thing that matters here — that SMBs both *pay* and *stay*.
- **Leading indicators:** activation rate (48h first quote), quotes-sent/dealer/week, leads-converted-to-orders (the money-in proof), free→paid %.

## 6. First 90 Days (solo founder, realistic)

### Phase 1 — Prove activation & money-in (Wk 1-4)
| Wk | Actions | Targets |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build 100-dealer prospect list (file 05). Ship WhatsApp-quoting MVP + catalog load + lead inbox. | List + MVP live |
| 2 | Onboard 8-10 pilot dealers personally (load their catalogs FOR them). Hand-route real homeowner leads to them. | **8-10 free, activated** |
| 3 | Watch usage daily. Fix activation friction. Get first "I won a kitchen from your lead" story. | ≥1 order attributed |
| 4 | Introduce paywall to pilots who got value. Measure first free→paid. | **2-4 paying** |

### Phase 2 — Tighten the loop (Wk 5-8)
| Wk | Actions | Targets |
|---|---|---|
| 5-6 | Scale outreach (10/day). Standardize onboarding to <30 min. | **40 free, 20 activated** |
| 7-8 | Push Habit→Paying conversions; sell lead add-ons. Track churn honestly. | **6-8 paying, ₹15-30k MRR** |

### Phase 3 — Establish repeatability (Wk 9-13)
| Wk | Actions | Targets |
|---|---|---|
| 9-11 | Expand to city #2-3. Annual prepay push. Document the free→paid playbook. | **65-80 free, 25-30 activated** |
| 12-13 | Lock unit economics: free→paid %, monthly churn, money-in per dealer. Decide GREEN/YELLOW. | **8-12 paying, ₹20-50k MRR** |

### 90-Day Scorecard (and the GREEN test)
- ✅ 60-80 free signups, 25-30 activated
- ✅ **8-12 paying dealers, ₹20-50k MRR**
- ✅ Measured **free→paid ≥30-40%** among activated dealers — *if hit, this clears to GREEN*
- ✅ Monthly paid churn ≤8%
- ✅ ≥3 dealers with a documented order won via Cubekrafts lead (money-in proof)

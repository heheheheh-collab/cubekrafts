## 0. The council's honest verdict on the pivot (READ THIS FIRST)

You asked for honesty, not cheerleading. Here it is. We re-ran the full council on the pivot. The result was **not a green light** — it was a sharp warning followed by one narrow escape route.

| Lens | Verdict | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| 🔬 Research | 🔴 **RED** | A furnishings *aggregator* dies — the leaders are vertically integrated and won't list; only low-trust carpenters would, i.e. adverse selection. |
| ⚙️ Technical | 🟡 **YELLOW** | Buildable solo **only** if cut to one category, 2D, with a correct server-side pricing engine. RED if you attempt a full 3D configurator. |
| 📈 Sales | 🟡 **YELLOW** | Demand CAC is *worse* than prefab — Livspace/HomeLane own the keywords. Exactly one wedge works. |
| 🎖️ COO (me) | 🟡 **YELLOW, conditional** | Bigger market, brutal category. Survives only as a capital-light wedge, never as "another interiors marketplace." |

**The blunt synthesis:** The pivot as literally stated — *"a marketplace for modular kitchens, wardrobes, bars, etc."* — is **a financing dead-end.** To a seed investor, "horizontal interiors marketplace" reads as "me-too Livspace," and that category has already incinerated nine figures of USD while still losing money (Livspace: ~₹1,400 cr revenue FY25, **still ~₹131 cr loss**; HomeLane: ₹756 cr FY25, still chasing profitability). A solo, no-budget founder cannot win a demand-gen war against that.

**BUT** all three reports independently surfaced the *same* survivable wedge. That wedge is the rest of this document.

> ### The one wedge that survives scrutiny
> **Modular KITCHENS only · factory-direct price transparency · Tier-2 cities first.**
> Incumbents charge **25–45% more** than local factory-direct dealers for *identical* hardware (Hettich/Hafele/Ebco). You don't compete with Livspace's design brand — you undercut their *price*, in cities they barely serve, with a value prop they structurally cannot copy without cannibalizing their margins: **"Same brands, 20–30% cheaper. Compare 3 vetted local quotes."**
> This also fixes Research's adverse-selection problem: the supply that *wants* to list is **factory-direct SME manufacturers hungry for leads**, not random carpenters.

**A second, even more honest option you should weigh** (Research's recommendation): *don't fully abandon prefab.* Prefab had real, uncrowded whitespace and was capital-light; furnishings is proven-but-bloodied. The mature move is to bring investors a transparent **prefab-vs-furnishings decision memo** rather than quietly pivoting. See §6 — this is a call only you can make.

## 1. The one-paragraph narrative (the survivable version)

> India's modular-kitchen market is large and growing double digits, but homeowners face a brutal trade-off: trust a **25–45% price premium** from a branded player (Livspace, HomeLane) or gamble on an **unvetted local carpenter** with no quotes, no comparison, and no recourse. Outside the top metros there is **no trusted, price-transparent way to buy a modular kitchen.** **Cubekrafts is the price-transparency layer for modular kitchens in Tier-2 India** — homeowners get 3 vetted, factory-direct quotes ("same brands, 20–30% cheaper"); dealers get high-intent local leads + the quoting/catalog tooling they completely lack. Asset-light, monetized by the dealers.

That is your elevator pitch. Notice what it is **not**: it is not "a marketplace for all modular furniture." Narrow wins the raise.

- **Sign 15–20 dealers**, free to list, pay-per-qualified-lead.

### 🟡 Weeks 4–8 — Turn on demand + thin product slice
- Make the **instant kitchen cost calculator** the hero of the landing page, gated behind name+phone+city.
- Tiny Google/Meta pilot on **"modular kitchen price [Tier-2 city]"** — deliberately *off* the metro keywords incumbents saturate. Measure CAC.
- Ship the **minimum real domain model**: Dealer, Quote, KitchenLead + a **correct, server-side, version-stamped pricing engine** (the CTO's #1 risk — the displayed price *is* the quote; get the math right before any UI polish). 2D only. No 3D/AR.

### 🟢 Weeks 8–13 — Package the raise
- Convert 3–5 dealers to a paid tier using lead proof → first revenue line.
- Land ≥1 documented **closed kitchen** (the killer slide).
- Build the deck (`04`), the metrics dashboard (§3), the data room (the reports + this doc).
- Start investor conversations.

**Deliberately do NOT build:** 3D/AR configurator, wardrobes/bars/other categories, dealer self-serve onboarding, payments, faceted search. The CTO ranked these P3/deferred — they are *post-raise*. Building them solo burns the 90 days. (Full reasoning: `02-technical-audit.md` §0 & roadmap.)

# Cubekrafts — Social Media Launch Plan

**Prepared by:** COO · **For:** Tarun (solo, ~45–60 min/day budget, ₹0 ad spend to start)
**Job of social here:** feed the lead engine and the dealer pipeline — not collect followers. Every post ends at **cubekrafts.com/get-quotes** (homeowners) or **cubekrafts.com** dealer signup (dealers).

---

## 1. Strategy in one paragraph

Two audiences, two motions. **Homeowners** (demand) live on Instagram/Facebook/YouTube and buy with their eyes — they get design + price-transparency content in the brand's editorial look. **Dealers** (supply) live on WhatsApp and watch LinkedIn — they get founder-led "we bring you business" proof. A solo founder wins with **founder-face content batched weekly**, not a faceless brand page posting daily. City-first: everything geo-tagged and captioned to your launch cities (Mumbai/Pune first) — 500 local followers beat 50,000 random ones.

## 2. Channels — ruthless priority order

| Priority | Channel | Audience | Why / Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **WhatsApp** (Business profile + Status + local groups) | Dealers + warm homeowners | India's real conversion layer. Status = your daily soft channel; Business catalog = mini-storefront; the quote-share loop already markets you. |
| 2 | **Instagram** (@cubekrafts) | Homeowners 25–45 | Reels = free reach in home/interior niche. The catalog designs + price transparency are made for it. |
| 3 | **LinkedIn** (your personal profile, not a company page) | Dealers' owners, investors, partners | Founder build-in-public. This is also your fundraise surface. |
| 4 | **Facebook** (page + local groups) | Homeowners 30–55, housing societies | Apartment/society groups and "Home interior ideas India" groups are where real kitchen budgets ask for recommendations. |
| 5 | **YouTube Shorts** (repost Reels) | Search-adjacent homeowners | Free second life for the same videos; "kitchen cost" content ranks for years. |
| ❌ | X/Twitter, Pinterest, Threads | — | Skip until there's a team. Width kills solo founders. |

Also (not social, but do it week 1): **Google Business Profile** for Cubekrafts — local searches convert better than any post.

## 3. Content pillars (the only 4 things we talk about)

1. **Price transparency (hero pillar)** — "What does a 10×8 L-shaped kitchen ACTUALLY cost in Mumbai?" Real sq-ft math, laminate vs acrylic vs PU, where dealers pad quotes. Nobody in the space shows honest numbers; we're built on them.
2. **Design drops** — catalog items as swipeable carousels/Reels: "Coastal Whites L-Kitchen · ₹1.8–2.5L · get 3 quotes." Direct feeder to /catalog.
3. **Behind the trade (founder-led)** — build-in-public: "Why local dealers quote 25–40% less than big brands for the same Hettich hardware." Dealer spotlights ("Meet Sharma Kitchens, 200 kitchens in 12 years"). Trust content for BOTH sides.
4. **How it works / proof** — 30-sec screen recordings: homeowner request → verified lead → 2-minute quote → WhatsApp. Later: real quotes going out, review screenshots, "this week on Cubekrafts" numbers (real ones only — never inflate; same rule as the site).

**Voice:** the site's editorial calm — helpful, numeric, unhyped. "Same brands. Fair price. Three quotes." No 🔥🔥🔥, no "revolutionizing."
**Look:** forest green + brass + linen, Kraft Seal on every asset. Make a 4-template kit in Canva once (price-breakdown card, design drop, founder note, proof/screenshot frame) and reuse forever.

## 4. Launch sequence — 4 weeks

### Week 0 (setup, one evening)
- Claim handles (@cubekrafts everywhere), seal as avatar, lockup as cover, link → cubekrafts.com/get-quotes.
- WhatsApp Business: catalog with 4–6 designs, quick replies, status intro post.
- Build the 4 Canva templates. Record 3 screen-demo clips of the product once, reuse endlessly.
- Google Business Profile submitted.

### Week 1 — Soft launch (founder story)
- **LinkedIn:** the origin post — "India's modular furniture trade runs on WhatsApp and guesswork. I built Cubekrafts to fix the quote." Product screenshots, honest pre-launch framing, ask for intros to kitchen dealers. (This post also warms investors.)
- **Instagram/FB:** 3 posts — brand intro, first design drop, first price-breakdown card.
- **WhatsApp Status:** daily — building the product, first designs.
- Personally invite 30–50 contacts to follow; join 5 local FB society/interior groups (just join, don't pitch yet).

### Week 2 — Public launch
- **Launch Reel** (the flagship 30s): screen-record the loop — browse design → get 3 quotes → dealer quotes in 2 min → WhatsApp. Caption: "Getting a modular kitchen quote in [city] used to take a week. Now it takes a day. Free for homeowners." Post everywhere same day; ask 10 friends to share within the first hour.
- **LinkedIn launch post:** the "it's live" moment + the dealer offer (founding dealers: free leads, ₹499/mo lock-in later).
- First **helpful** (not salesy) answer in FB groups: someone asks "kitchen contractor recommendation?" → give genuine advice + "I run a free service that gets you 3 vetted local quotes."
- 3 Reels this week: price breakdown, design drop, "how it works."

### Week 3 — Price-transparency blitz (the differentiator)
- Reel series: "Kitchen pricing in India, explained" — Part 1: why quotes vary 3×; Part 2: laminate vs acrylic vs PU per sq ft; Part 3: the questions that expose a padded quote; Part 4: hardware brands decoded (Hettich/Hafele/Ebco).
- Same content threaded on LinkedIn as a "what I'm learning building this" post.
- Dealer spotlight #1 (with their permission — they'll reshare to their own network, which recruits other dealers).

### Week 4 — Proof + rhythm
- "First month, real numbers" post (only true numbers: requests received, quotes sent, cities). Vulnerable + credible beats fake big.
- Review/quote screenshots as proof frames.
- Settle into the **sustainable weekly cadence:** 3 Reels (IG+FB+YT Shorts), 1 carousel, 1 LinkedIn founder post, daily WhatsApp Status, 2–3 genuine group answers. ≈ one 2-hour batching session + 20 min/day.

## 5. City playbook (repeat per launch city)
Geo-tag everything; captions name the city ("Thinking of a modular kitchen in **Pune**?"); join that city's society/interior FB groups; run the dealer spotlight from that city. When a city's dealer cohort activates, its content calendar starts — social follows supply, not the other way around.

## 6. Paid (only after organic proves the hook)
Hold ₹0 for 4–6 weeks. Then: **boost the single best-performing Reel** ₹3–5k in one city targeting 25–50 home-interest audiences → measure cost per /get-quotes submission against the ₹300–800 CPL benchmark in doc 08. Scale only what beats it. WhatsApp click-to-chat ads are the likely winner long-term (dealers AND homeowners both convert in chat) — test at week 8+.

## 7. Metrics that matter (weekly, 5 minutes)

| Metric | Target by day 30 |
|---|---|
| ⭐ /get-quotes submissions from social (ask "where did you hear about us?" in the founder-verification call) | 15–25 |
| Dealer signups/DMs from LinkedIn+WhatsApp | 5–10 conversations |
| IG: Reel views + saves (saves = intent) | 1–2 Reels >2k views |
| WhatsApp Status views (your warm-audience pulse) | growing weekly |

**Vanity to ignore:** follower counts, likes, posting streaks. One homeowner request beats 500 likes.

## 8. Rules (same integrity bar as the site)
1. **Never invent numbers, reviews, or testimonials.** Real or nothing — same rule that got the fake traction card deleted.
2. Every asset carries the seal, the city, and one CTA.
3. Founder face > logo. People follow people; brands earn follows later.
4. Batch weekly; never let social eat dealer-call hours — **outreach and follow-ups outrank posting every day of the week.**
5. Reply to every comment/DM within 24h — social is the top of the same trust funnel the whole company runs on.

---

*Social's job in the next 90 days is modest and clear: a steady trickle of verified homeowner requests, a warm surface for dealer recruiting, and a public build-log an investor can scroll. The launch Reel matters less than the 12th consecutive honest week.*

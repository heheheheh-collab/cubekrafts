## Slide 2 — The Problem

### Buying a prefab home in India is a leap of faith.

- **No common language** — "modular," "prefab," "PEB," "LGSF" all mean different things. You can't compare like-for-like.
- **Opaque pricing** — quotes swing from ₹1,000 to ₹4,000/sq ft with unclear inclusions.
- **No neutral quality signal** — every vendor claims "best, durable, ISO-certified."
- **Fear of disappearance** — a ₹20–40 lakh one-time purchase with no recourse if the vendor vanishes.
- **No advisor** — first-time buyers have no architect or contractor to guide them.

**Speaker note:** "Manufacturers are everywhere. The problem isn't supply — it's that buyers have no way to choose with confidence."

---

## Slide 3 — Why Now

### Three forces make this the moment.

- 🏛️ **Government tailwind** — PMAY-U 2.0: 1 crore urban homes, ₹10 lakh crore allocated. Housing shortage 10M → 25–31M units by 2030.
- ⚡ **Prefab is winning** — ~2× faster to build, 10–25% cheaper than conventional. Material & labor inflation widens the gap.
- 📱 **Buyers are already searching** — high-intent queries like "prefab house cost per sq ft" are surging, with no neutral destination to land on.

**Speaker note:** "The demand is structural and the search behavior already exists. Nobody owns the destination."

## Slide 6 — The Wedge

### We start where the pain — and the willingness to pay — is highest.

## Slide 7 — Business Model

### Asset-light. We monetize the introduction, not the construction.

**Buyers: 100% free.** Manufacturers pay, three stacked lines:

| Line | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Pay-per-qualified-lead** | ₹250–600 / lead | The wedge — lands skeptics, no upfront cost |
| **Subscription tiers** | ₹0 / ₹4,999 / ₹12,999 per month | Recurring MRR once they've seen lead value |
| **Featured placement** | ₹2,999–7,999 / month | High-margin add-on |

*The cost estimator is the funnel engine, not a revenue line.*

**Speaker note:** "We deliberately avoid the capital-heavy commission/full-stack-builder path. We stay neutral and asset-light."

## Slide 11 — Go-To-Market

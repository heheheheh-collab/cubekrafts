> **Fit labels:** **⭐ ICP** = growing 2-10 person dealer likely losing quotes — feels the pain, can pay (pilot these first). **🟢 ANCHOR** = larger/regional brand — credibility logo, may already have systems (sell later / case-study value). **▫ EDGE** = smaller/likely one-person — free-tier only, low WTP, not a paid target.

---

## How to work this list (SaaS sales motion)

**Daily cadence (solo):** ~10 fresh prospects/day + follow-ups (~50 touches/week). It's a countable B2B list now — run it like a pipeline, not a spray.

**Order of attack:**
1. **⭐ ICP dealers first** — those with a team (2-10), active lead-gen, manual quoting. Pilot, onboard personally, load their catalog FOR them, route a real lead, then convert to paid.
2. **🟢 Anchors** for credibility/case studies — pursue in parallel, expect slower and possibly "we have a system."
3. **Mine IndiaMART/TradeIndia/McCoy** directory URLs to extend to 100+ ICP prospects.

**Qualify each prospect on:** team size ≥2, already buys leads (Meta/Justdial/IndiaMART), quotes manually in Excel/PDF/WhatsApp, complains about follow-up leakage. Those are your highest free→paid odds.

**CRM table schema (paste into a sheet):**

| company | city | product | contact | channel | fit | status | last_touch | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| _Name_ | _City_ | _kitchens / wardrobes / both_ | _Phone/email once verified_ | _Website / IndiaMART / Justdial_ | _ICP / anchor / edge_ | _new → contacted → signed_up → activated → paying → churned_ | _YYYY-MM-DD_ | _team size, lead-gen, pain notes_ |

`status` for SaaS: `new` → `contacted` → `signed_up (free)` → `activated (first quote)` → `paying` → (`churned`).
